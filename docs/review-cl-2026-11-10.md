### Not fixed (known gaps, low risk for the demo)
- **`STT_MODE=turn`:** `TurnBasedStt` calls `transcribe_rest` inside voice-core, outside the app gate. The default `stream` mode is not affected. Syncing voice-core from dadi-vs-scammers would bring its own gate (`gate.gnani_slot()`); the app gate nests safely inside it.
- **Two speakers:** only a heuristic catches this (SNR plus a non-empty Prisma transcript). A clean recording of two people talking in turn passes. The UI asks for "only your voice".
- **Design §7:** when the clone call rejects a language, the app does not fall back to the closest language. It falls back to the Timbre voice and shows the badge, which is acceptable.
- **Screen readers:** the scanning highlight is visual only (no `aria-current`). Fine for switch users; a blind user would not hear it.
- **Old service worker:** a phone that installed the old worker may show the old build on its first load after the deploy. A second reload fixes it, or clear the site data once before the demo.
- **LLM privacy:** the profile's name, family names and condition, plus the partner's captions (text, not audio), go to Groq for reply suggestions. This is by design (`02-design.md` §5) but worth one line in the privacy story.

## Verified fine
- **Consent, end to end:** the API returns 400 without `consent=true` (also `consent=false`) and 422 for a bad clip. "Build my voice" stays disabled until consent is ticked. `consent_at` is stored on enrolment and cleared on delete. The embedding is never returned to the browser (`public()` drops `voice`).
- **Privacy:** `.env`, `cache/` (SQLite profile, recordings, TTS cache) and `recordings/` are git-ignored. `git ls-files` lists no audio, database or key files and no personal data. Audio leaves only for Gnani (clip transcript, embedding, TTS). Banked messages are stored and replayed locally, with no AI.
- **Review focus:**
  1. A broken `.opus` upload shows "Please export as MP3/WAV" and a "Can't read" badge (checked in Chrome). A real Safari `.opus` still needs a live check.
  2. Music or noise is flagged by the SNR check (`test_noisy_clip_low_snr`). No clear speech is flagged by the Prisma transcript (new `test_clip_with_no_clear_speech_is_flagged`).
  3. Clone failure → Timbre fallback (`test_clone_failure_falls_back_to_timbre`). The badge works: in Chrome it read "Your voice", then "Fallback voice" after delete.
  4. Echo: every utterance (Speak, replies, SOS) is noted on all live sessions, and the echo transcript is dropped (`test_echo_dropped_partner_captioned_with_suggestions`).
  5. Romanised Hindi is rewritten to Devanagari before cloning (`test_romanised_hindi_is_rewritten_then_cloned`).
- **Accessibility:** phrase tiles are 64 px (≥ 56) in 2 columns at 390 px. Suggestion chips are 60 px, the Speak button 64 px, the SOS button 260 px. Focus rings are visible. Scanning works with Space, Enter or the big Select button, and typing in text boxes doesn't trigger it. Icon-only buttons have `aria-label`s. Captions are `aria-live`.
- **Converse (fake mode, Chrome with a fake mic):** captions appear, 3 suggestions follow each caption, and the mic stops when you leave the page.
- **Layout:** no horizontal overflow on any page at 390 px (`scrollWidth` check). The desktop layout at 1440 px is clean.
- **Prisma streaming** gets one locale (`indic-auto` when a list is passed), as the live finding requires.

## Ranked live-test checklist (needs real keys)
1. **Gate under load:** with a cloned voice, double-tap Speak, press the scanning Select while a phrase plays, and Speak while a clip check runs. The backend log should have no failing 429 (a logged "retry" is fine) and the badge should stay "Your voice".
2. **Real voice notes:** a 20–60 s WhatsApp `.opus` on Android Chrome and on iPhone Safari. Expect "Good" for a long note now. If Safari can't decode it, check the "Please export as MP3/WAV" message.
3. **Delete my voice:** after delete, `cache/clone/tts` is empty and Speak shows "Fallback voice".
4. **SOS failure path:** with a bad `GNANI_API_KEY` (or the server stopped), SOS shows the text in large type and the phone reads it.
5. **Converse on speaker:** your own reply is not captioned (echo), suggestions arrive in under 2 s, and Groq stays under ~8k tokens/min over 5 minutes of talk.
6. **Cross-language and romanised:** "mujhe paani chahiye" in Hindi, a Tamil sentence in the cloned voice, and one language without a phrase list (kn-IN shows English tiles, spoken in Kannada).
7. **Switch scanning on a phone:** with a Bluetooth keyboard or switch, the highlight stays visible above the tab bar.
8. **Phone that opened the old build:** reload twice and confirm the new UI (the "My recordings" card on Profile).
